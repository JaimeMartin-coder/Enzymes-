Part 2: Temp check 🌡️ Don't get Enzo cooked 🔒 Badge

DCI: LS1.A Structure and Function CCC: Cause and Effect CCC: Patterns SEP: Analyzing and Interpreting Data
Mission: Find the temperature where this enzyme works best, and cook it on purpose to see what happens when it gets too hot.

How to play

  1. Slide the temperature to a spot you want to test. Watch the timer. When it turns green, press Record dot. Each dot needs a different temperature.
  2. Must do: cook the enzyme. Record 1 dot at a HIGH temperature where the enzymes turn gray (denature) 💀.
  3. Your choice: record 4 more dots at temperatures where the enzymes still work. Spread them out so you can see the whole shape of the graph.
  4. If enzymes turn gray (RIP 💀), press Fresh enzymes. The slider resets to a safe 25 °C. Wait for the timer, then keep going.
  5. You only get ONE graph, so choose wisely. When all 5 dots are done, press Draw line of best fit and answer the 2 questions about your graph.

Part 3: pH vibe check 🧪 🔒 Badge

DCI: LS1.A Structure and Function CCC: Stability and Change CCC: Cause and Effect SEP: Planning and Carrying Out Investigations
Mission: Find the best pH for your enzyme, and denature it on BOTH sides: a pH that is too low and a pH that is too high.

pH tells you how acidic something is. Low numbers are acids (like stomach acid). 7 is neutral (like water). High numbers are bases.

How to play

  1. Pick an enzyme, then slide the pH to a spot you want to test. Watch the timer. When it turns green, press Record dot. Each dot needs a different pH.
  2. Must do (2 dots): denature the enzyme at a pH that is too low AND at a pH that is too high 💀. The enzymes turn gray when they are denatured.
  3. Your choice: record 4 more dots at pH values where the enzymes still work. Spread them out so you can find the peak.
  4. If enzymes turn gray (RIP 💀), press Fresh enzymes. The slider resets to a safe pH 5. Wait for the timer, then keep going.
  5. You only get ONE graph, so choose wisely. When all 6 dots are done, press Draw line of best fit and answer the 2 questions about your graph.
